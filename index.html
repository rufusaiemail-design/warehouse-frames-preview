Our Story

Family owned.
Houston proud.

Warehouse Frames 'n Gallery has been serving Houston for over 27 years. What started as a local framing shop has grown into a 20,000 square foot destination — the largest selection of ready-made frames in the Houston area.

We're open to the public and trade. Interior designers, decorators, commercial accounts, and walk-in customers are all welcome. No appointment needed. Just come in and see it for yourself.
